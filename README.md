# Somast
**Building the Foundations of Somali AI.**

Somali is spoken by an estimated 20+ million people, yet it remains a low-resource language in AI, with limited datasets, benchmarks, and tools compared to its speaker base. Somast is an open research lab working to close that gap, building open datasets, language technologies, developer tools, and educational resources for Somali AI.

---

## Our Focus
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Speech Technology (STT & TTS)
- Open Datasets & Corpora
- Language Tools
